---
name: Tulai business rule
description: Difference between Tulai and Kata and the user's requirement to preserve Kata.
---

Tulai is manual weighing done by Himmal and is collected to be paid to Himmal. Kata is weighing done by a heavy machine and is income for the cold store owner.

Tulai is an optional sale charge; null means zero. The user wants the same downstream billing and calculation impacts as sale-level Kata, without changing Kata or anything associated with it.

Accrue the matching Tulai labour expense when it is billed. Provide a separate Tulai expense item, with the total unpaid amount in brackets, payable through the Cash Revenue Expense tab.

Always keep Tulai columns and variables immediately after Kata so the parallel is easy to follow. Include Tulai Charges in the Kata CSV, immediately after Kata Charges.

Use only तुलाई as the Hindi label, without the bracketed description.

In individual sale forms, the English label should be "Tulai (Manual Weighing)".

On Cold Storage and Sales receipts, always label Tulai as तुलाई like the other Hindi charge rows, regardless of the app language.

The user's receipt requirement covers both actual A4 printing and the generated/shared PDF: keep both individual bill types on one page, retaining existing bottom payment entries. Longer batch/history outputs must not lose rows to force a single page.

**Why:** The user stated these business rules and explained that Tulai is currently managed through workarounds.

**How to apply:** Keep Tulai separate from Kata. Include its sale-charge effects throughout the relevant calculations and outputs. Recognise Tulai labour cost at billing time and do not count its cash payout again as a P&L expense. Standalone Kata collections remain outside this feature's scope.
